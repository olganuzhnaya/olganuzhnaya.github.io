Introduction: A New Way to Think About Money

Money has always been more than a means of payment. It is also a technology of trust — a system through which people exchange value, keep records, settle obligations and coordinate economic activity.

Cryptocurrency introduces a different approach to digital value. Instead of relying exclusively on traditional financial institutions, some digital asset networks use cryptography, distributed ledgers and software protocols to record and transfer value.

To understand cryptocurrency, it is necessary to look beyond market prices and examine the technology, economics, ownership structures, risks and legal environment surrounding digital assets.

What Is Cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency is a broad term for digital assets that use cryptographic techniques as part of the mechanisms used to secure transactions, control access or maintain the integrity of records.

Different cryptocurrencies have different technical designs, monetary policies, governance structures and economic purposes. Bitcoin and Ethereum, for example, are built around significantly different approaches.
