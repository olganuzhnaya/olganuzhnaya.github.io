Technology

Solana is a blockchain network designed for high-throughput decentralized applications. The network uses Proof of Stake together with a mechanism called Proof of History to help coordinate the ordering of events.

History

Solana was founded by Anatoly Yakovenko and was launched as a blockchain project in the late 2010s. The Solana mainnet beta launched in 2020.
