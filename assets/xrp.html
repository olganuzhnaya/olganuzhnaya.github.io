Technology

The XRP Ledger is a decentralized blockchain that uses the XRP Ledger Consensus Protocol to validate transactions. The network is designed to process transactions quickly without relying on Proof of Work mining.

History

The XRP Ledger was launched in 2012. XRP was created as the native digital asset of the XRP Ledger. The technology has been developed and used for applications involving payments and digital asset transfers.
