Why Bitcoin has a maximum supply of 21 million

Bitcoin's protocol defines a maximum supply of 21 million BTC. New bitcoins are created through mining rewards. The reward decreases over time through events known as halvings. This mechanism creates a predictable issuance schedule built into the protocol.

Bitcoin Technology

History of Bitcoin

In 2008, a person or group using the name Satoshi Nakamoto published the Bitcoin whitepaper "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System". The first Bitcoin block, known as the Genesis Block, was mined in January 2009.

Bitcoin is based on a distributed ledger called blockchain. Transactions are grouped into blocks and secured through cryptographic methods. The Bitcoin network uses Proof-of-Work consensus, where miners compete to solve computational problems and add new blocks to the blockchain.
