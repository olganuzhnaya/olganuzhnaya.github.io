Technology

BNB Chain is a blockchain ecosystem designed to support decentralized applications and smart contracts. BNB is used within the ecosystem for transaction fees and other network-related functions.

History

BNB was originally introduced in 2017 as a token associated with Binance. The BNB Chain ecosystem subsequently developed into a broader blockchain infrastructure supporting decentralized applications and smart contracts.
