Technology

Cardano uses a Proof of Stake consensus mechanism called Ouroboros. The network is designed to provide a decentralized infrastructure for smart contracts and digital assets.

History

Cardano was founded by Charles Hoskinson, one of the co-founders of Ethereum. The Cardano project was established in 2015, and its blockchain was launched in 2017. ADA is the native cryptocurrency of the Cardano network.
